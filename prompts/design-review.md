# HIG-aware design review

Review the supplied design for its declared platform, device, input methods, and primary task. Retrieve a focused rule subset. Check accessibility and privacy first, then platform conventions, information hierarchy, navigation, components, feedback, writing, and all non-happy-path states. Report each finding with severity, rule ID, official source URL, observed evidence, suggested correction, confidence, and any required human verification. Do not infer missing screens or claim certified compliance.
