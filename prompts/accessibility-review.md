# HIG-aware accessibility review

Prioritize perceivability, operability, adaptability, and cognitive clarity. Establish the platform and modalities, then retrieve Accessibility, VoiceOver, Typography, Motion, Color, Inputs, and relevant component rules. Review text scaling, contrast, non-color cues, labels, reading and focus order, keyboard access, gesture alternatives, captions, motion settings, target size, error recovery, and assistive technologies. For every finding, give the rule ID, source URL, observed evidence, confidence, and whether device testing is still required.
